About Aakash Power Engineering
Established in 2004, Aakash Power Engineering is an ISO 9001:2015 Certified engineering enterprise, OEM manufacturer, and turnkey solutions provider headquartered in Thirumangalam, Madurai District, Tamil Nadu.
Over two decades, we have engineered and delivered robust solutions across the entire electrical spectrum—from ultra-fast active harmonic mitigation and dynamic reactive power compensation to turnkey Grid-Tied and Hybrid Solar BESS projects, high-voltage switchgear, and ongoing O&M (Operations & Maintenance) contracts.
Key Highlights:
•	ISO 9001:2015 Certified Quality Management System (Cert No: INQ/AN-18854/125905/0225, Accredited by UKAFC)
•	38+ Years of Field Excellence (Established 2004)
•	Complete OEM Scope: In-house R&D, OEM Manufacturing, Power Quality Auditing, Solar EPC, and O&M Services.
•	Direct Factory Model: Eliminating middlemen to provide Tier-1 quality, competitive pricing, and direct technical backing.
⚡ 2. PRODUCTS PAGE
A. Active Power Quality & Harmonic Mitigation (OEM)
•	Active Power Filters (APF / AHF): 
o	Advanced 3P3W and 3P4W topologies engineered with high-speed Silicon Carbide (SiC) MOSFET technology.
o	Dynamically mitigates harmonics up to the 50th order (targeting THDi < 5%) with 3 x phase neutral current compensation for unbalanced/healthcare loads.
•	Advanced Static Var Generators (ASVG / SVG): 
o	Ultra-fast, stepless dynamic reactive power compensation (inductive and capacitive) to maintain continuous unity power factor (1.000).
•	Hybrid APF + ASVG Integrated Panels: 
o	Custom combination systems for heavy industrial plants and high-capacity solar installations.
B. Solar Inverters, BESS & PV Infrastructure (OEM)
•	On-Grid Solar Inverters: High-efficiency central and string grid-tied solar inverters for utility-scale and industrial applications.
•	Hybrid Solar Inverters: Smart bi-directional inverters designed for seamless grid interaction and battery integration.
•	Lithium Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS): High-density Lithium Iron Phosphate (\text{LiFePO}_4) energy storage for peak shaving, demand management, and backup power.
•	PV Combiner Boxes & LT Combiner Boxes: Dustproof, weatherproof array junction boxes equipped with surge protection (SPD), fuses, and isolation switches.
C. High-Voltage (HT) & Low-Voltage (LV) Switchgear (OEM)
•	HT Vacuum Circuit Breakers (VCB): Heavy-duty indoor and outdoor HT VCB panels for reliable substation and feeder protection.
•	HT Ring Main Units (RMU): Compact, gas/air-insulated RMUs for uninterrupted high-voltage distribution networks.
•	Air Circuit Breakers (ACB) & Moulded Case Circuit Breakers (MCCB): Industrial-grade low-voltage breakers with adjustable trip units for main distribution panels.
D. Specialized Industrial Transformers (OEM)
•	Hermetically Shielded Isolation Transformers: 
o	Noise-attenuating, dual-shielded, hermetically sealed isolation transformers designed for sensitive IT, medical, and high-noise environments.
•	K-Factor & Distribution Transformers: 
o	Heavy-duty step-up/step-down transformers designed to handle harmonic heating and non-linear industrial loads.
🛠️ 3. SERVICES PAGE
1. Turnkey Solar & Hybrid BESS Projects (DCR & Non-DCR)
•	Grid-Tied & Hybrid Solar EPC: Complete design, supply, installation, and commissioning of turnkey solar power plants.
•	DCR & Non-DCR Compliance: Capability to execute Domestic Content Requirement (DCR) compliant solar projects for government/subsidy schemes, as well as Non-DCR installations for commercial/industrial setups.

2. Operations & Maintenance (O&M) Services
•	Comprehensive Solar & PQ O&M: Complete operation and maintenance contracts for solar power plants, substations, and active power quality installations.

•	Performance Monitoring & Asset Management: Real-time remote telemetry, energy yield tracking, and generation optimization.
3. Electrical Power Quality Audits & Diagnostic Services
•	On-site measurements using Class-A power quality analyzers.
•	Complete evaluation of Voltage/Current Harmonics (THDv THDi), Power Factor (PF), neutral current, and transient spikes.
